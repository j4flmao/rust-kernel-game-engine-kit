# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, malicious disclosure of private
information, and deliberately unsafe behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when conduct violates this policy. Report concerns privately to
the maintainers rather than escalating them in public threads.
